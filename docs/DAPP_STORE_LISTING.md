## Description（英文）

Click+1 is a one-tap number puzzle. Tap a tile to increase its value, merge three or more connected equal tiles, and build bigger numbers through cascading combinations. Each move uses energy, and each merge restores energy. Plan your moves and aim for a higher score.

Try the puzzle in guest mode, or connect a compatible Solana wallet to sign in and keep your game with your account. English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese interfaces are available. Wallet gameplay includes a daily free undo; additional paid undos cost 0.01 SOL plus network fees. The price is shown before you approve a payment.

Click+1 offers no wagering, investment returns, token rewards, or cash prizes. Confirmed blockchain payments are normally irreversible, subject to applicable rights and support handling for duplicate charges or service failures.
